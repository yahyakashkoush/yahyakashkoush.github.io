---
name: Portfolio visual direction
description: User's requested direction for the portfolio redesign
---

The user asked to improve the portfolio after criticism of its colors, suggested black, and delegated judgment: «اعمل الصح الاحترافي بدون مايبان ai».

**Why:** The user wants the portfolio to look professional rather than AI-generated.

**How to apply:** Keep a dark, professional foundation and clear content; do not restore the previous red glow and excessive effects during later maintenance.

The user later said the all-gray version felt too muted: «حاسس الموقع بقي مطفي اوي ظبط الاوان».

**Why:** A fully desaturated, heavily darkened treatment lost too much visual clarity and energy.

**How to apply:** Use brighter text, distinguish dark surfaces, and allow a limited accent color rather than insisting on a completely gray palette.

The user subsequently requested removal of all images and “monochrome with blue empesse color”, then delegated the exact professional, formal palette because a company will review the portfolio.

**Why:** The portfolio needs to make a formal professional impression for a company audience.

**How to apply:** Keep portraits, project screenshots and hover previews removed. Use neutral black/gray/white with restrained blue emphasis; this supersedes the earlier warm-sand accent.

The user explicitly requested the original television and its original video in the empty right side of the introduction.

**Why:** This is an intentional exception to the earlier removal of all imagery, not permission to restore the other images.

**How to apply:** Keep the original systems reel in a television beside the introduction on desktop and below the text on mobile. Do not restore the separate end-of-page television.

The user also explicitly asked to restore the old animated smoke background.

**Why:** They want this specific ambient effect back, despite preferring restrained presentation overall.

**How to apply:** Allow subtle neutral smoke behind the introduction, keeping text legible and preserving reduced-motion behavior. This does not permit restoring red glow or other removed effects.

The user asked to remove the photo beside “Architecture, not just models.” Keep that About section text-led rather than restoring its portrait.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removal of that image.

**How to apply:** Retain the heading and supporting content without a decorative photo in this section.

Coordinate the inquiry envelope with animation completion events rather than independent timers, and include page enter/exit duration when judging its total opening time.

**Why:** Independent stages can drift or leave the form delayed even when each individual movement looks correct.

**How to apply:** Keep letter, flap, and seal movements sequential, honor reduced motion, and show success only after the submission succeeds.
